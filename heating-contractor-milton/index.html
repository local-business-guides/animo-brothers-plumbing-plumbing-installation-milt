What to expect, from first call to completed work

A heating contractor visit generally starts with a conversation about the symptoms, the equipment, and when the problem began. The contractor can then assess the system and explain the recommended work. The details depend on the equipment and the issue, so don’t rely on a general description alone when deciding what your home needs.

Key facts to discuss:
- What it covers: assessment and heating work, with the exact repair or installation scope confirmed for your system.
- Timing: the visit and any follow-up work depend on the problem, scheduling, and whether parts or equipment are needed.
- Cost factors: the type of work, parts or equipment, access to the system, and repair versus replacement can affect the total. Ask for the scope and cost before approving work.
- Provider comparison: ask each contractor about experience with your equipment, what the proposed work includes, and whether follow-up is included. Compare written estimates on the same scope, not just the bottom line.

You may hear diagnosis, meaning the contractor’s assessment of what is causing the problem, or replacement, meaning removal of existing equipment and installation of new equipment. Ask the contractor to explain any unfamiliar term before work begins.
