Scope, planning, and useful terms

Start by describing the fixture or plumbing change you have in mind. The plumber may need to review the existing setup and the work area before confirming the scope. Explain any concerns, such as a fixture that does not work as expected or a previous repair that may affect the new work. Avoid assuming that a replacement will use the same connections or materials until the setup has been checked.

When comparing providers, look for a clear description of the work, the materials included, the expected schedule, and what could change the cost. Ask how the provider will communicate if the existing plumbing calls for additional work. These details help you compare proposals on the work itself, not just on a brief summary.

A few terms you may hear:
- Fixture: An item connected to the plumbing, such as a sink or toilet.
- Supply line: A pipe that brings water to a fixture.
- Drain line: A pipe that carries used water away.
- Scope: The agreed list of work included in the project.
